MAISIE Context Map

This map shows the nine areas (bounded contexts) inside MAISIE, what each one owns, and how each one connects to the others and to the outside systems it reads or writes.

The context map

Kinds of area

  • Core. The reason MAISIE exists: she turns Jack's day into billable hours and acts for him.
  • Supporting. Built for Jack's way of working, and it serves the core.
  • Generic. Plumbing that any assistant would need.
  • Outside system. MAISIE does not own its data.

Lines and labels

  • Green line, OHS (open host service). MAISIE Assistant uses this area through its shared tools.
  • ACLAnti-corruption layer. Code that translates an outside system's data into MAISIE's own terms.
  • CFConformist. MAISIE accepts the other side's model as it is.
  • C/SCustomer and supplier. One area depends on what the other area provides.
  • SKShared kernel. Two areas share one small part of the model.

Three points to keep in mind

Three areas are core.

MAISIE Assistant, Work Journal and Time & Billing together turn scattered work into invoices. A change there needs the most care and the most tests.

Billing data is not MAISIE's.

The NTA Time Tracker owns entries and invoices. MAISIE only adds unbilled entries, and she can delete only the entries she posted.

The Mac is a separate world.

The cloud reaches Mail and Calendar only through queues on the Mac. Contacts, the journal and calendar reads all depend on Mac jobs and macOS permissions.
